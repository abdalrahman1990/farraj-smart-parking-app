Delete Your Account

You can delete your Smart Parking account and all associated data (profile, vehicles, bookings, wallet history) in either way:

Option 1 – In the app (fastest)

Open the app → Profile → Delete Account → confirm. Your data is removed immediately.

Option 2 – Request by web

Fill the form below. It opens your email app with a pre-written deletion request to our support team. We process requests within 7 days and confirm by email.

Prefer email directly? Write to support@nextgen6th.com with subject “Delete my Smart Parking account” and include your account email.
