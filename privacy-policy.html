Privacy Policy – Farraj Smart Parking

Last updated: 15 September 2026

Farraj Smart Parking ("we", "our", "the app") provides smart parking booking and gate-control services. This policy explains what data we collect and how we use it.

1. Data we collect

  • Account data: name, email address, phone number, password (stored securely, never shared).
  • Vehicle data: vehicle nickname, brand, plate code and plate number — used to identify your vehicle at parking gates.
  • Location data: approximate GPS location, used only to show nearby parking locations. Collected only while the feature is used and never in the background.
  • Booking & payment data: reservations, wallet balance and payment history.
  • Device data: push-notification token (FCM) to send booking updates; basic device info for app stability.

2. How we use data

  • To create and manage your account, process bookings and wallet payments.
  • To operate parking gates (open/blink commands) linked to your reservations.
  • To send booking confirmations and service notifications.
  • To improve app reliability and prevent fraud. We do not sell your data.

3. Data sharing

Data is shared only with service providers strictly required to operate the app (cloud hosting, push notifications, payment processing), and with parking operators limited to the reservations at their locations. No advertising trackers are used.

4. Data retention & deletion

Your data is kept while your account is active. You can delete your account and all associated data at any time from Profile → Delete Account in the app, or by contacting us at the email below.

5. Security

All traffic uses encrypted HTTPS connections. Passwords are stored hashed and never in plain text.

6. Children's privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

7. Contact
